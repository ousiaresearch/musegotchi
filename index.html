What it is

It asks for things by telling you, not by showing you a bar — so you watch the pet, not a dashboard. It is hungry, it gets bored, it wants the light off to sleep, and once a day it asks you a question about your day, which only counts if your answer is different from yesterday's.

  • The streak is never shown to you. Nothing on screen displays it until the day is about to break, and then the pet just says so. It buys nothing: growth is the care count alone.
  • It never learns a fact about you. It keeps what you did, never anything about who you are. No export, no leaderboard, no network call anywhere in the file.
  • The window is not the door. Grow it on days 2, 4 and 8 if the care is good enough; miss the day and it says so, and can still catch up.

The long game

At day 8 it stops growing and becomes one of four forms. You do not pick it — you earn it, by how you kept the thing. The form is folded out of the log rather than stored, so a saved game can never disagree with it.

Steward

Kept clean. It tidies after itself.

Prospector

Kept playing. Restless, and pleased about it.

Clerk

Kept fed. Reads the day out in its own voice each morning.

Drifter

Nobody made a project of it, and it asks more often. Not a failure state.

The sound

Off by default, and it does not remember — close the page and it returns to off, on purpose. Twelve effects, one per thing you can do, and eight lo-fi rooms chosen by the pet's own state: a pet left alone overnight drifts into midnight by itself, and a neglected one gets firelight rather than something cheerful.

Its own voice is not a recording. It is generated from the pet's seed, so no two sound alike and it costs nothing to ship — and it cannot drift from its face or its name, because all three come from the same number.
