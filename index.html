Musegotchi

A small creature in a box. It is hungry, and it notices.

The three

The design is in what stays out.

01

The streak is never shown

It stays off every meter until the day is about to break. Then the pet says so. The number buys nothing; growth is the care count alone.

02

It learns no fact about you

It keeps what you did, never who you are. The daily answer becomes one rolling digest that can tell only whether today differs from yesterday.

03

The window is not the door

It can grow on days 2, 4 and 8 when its care is good enough. Miss the day and it tells you; good care can still let it catch up.

The four forms

What it becomes changes what it does.

At day 8 the pet stops growing. Its form is folded from the care log rather than stored, so a saved game cannot disagree with it.

Steward

Kept clean. It tidies after itself.

Prospector

Kept playing. It stays restless, and pleased about it.

Clerk

Kept fed. It reads the day out in its own voice each morning.

Drifter

Nobody made a project of it. It asks more often. This is not a failure state.

The sound

The room follows the pet.

12 effects, 8 rooms. Sound is off by default and never required. Leave the audio folder out and the game remains complete.

The room is chosen from the pet’s state, not the clock on your wall. A pet left alone overnight drifts into midnight; a neglected one gets firelight. Its voice is generated from the same seed as its face and name.

Limits

Before you judge it.

  • You cannot read the streak.It is deliberately edge-only, not a permanent meter.
  • A screenshot remains possible.The number is unshowable, not un-screenshottable; it is designed to be worthless to perform with.
  • The save is legible JSON.It sits in the browser’s Application storage. The game does not pretend otherwise.
  • An unreadable save means a fresh egg.Lost days are named without printing counts the game cannot know or accusing the caretaker.
